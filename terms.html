Official Hospitality Agreement Effective Date: November 2026

1. Agreement to Terms & Eligibility

By accessing, browsing, or placing a reservation via the Verdant Haven platform, you enter into a legally binding agreement governed by these Terms of Service. Guests must be at least 18 years of age and hold the legal capacity to enter contracts in their country of residence.

2. Reservations & Instant Confirmation

All bookings made through our portal generate an authenticated Booking ID (e.g., VH-XXXXX) and instant reservation voucher. Reservations are held under the name of the designated Lead Guest, who must present photo identification upon check-in at the farmstay.

3. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

Each property features an explicit cancellation policy displayed on its details card and booking invoice:

  • Flexible Policy: 100% full refund up to 5 days prior to scheduled check-in.
  • Moderate Policy: 100% full refund up to 7 days prior; 50% refund thereafter.
  • Strict Policy: 50% refund up to 14 days prior to arrival.

4. Living Farm Environment & Animal Welfare

Our partners are genuine operating family farms, organic apiaries, and equestrian ranches. Guests agree to treat all farm animals with kindness and follow farmer guidance during feeding or workshop activities. Children must be accompanied by adults near livestock pastures, ponds, and farm machinery.

5. House Rules, Quiet Hours & Damage

Quiet countryside hours apply between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM to preserve tranquility for neighboring estates. Smoking is strictly confined to designated outdoor gravel courtyards. Guests are responsible for reporting accidental damage immediately to the host or concierge desk.

Concierge Dispute Resolution
If you encounter any discrepancies upon arrival, our 24/7 guest hotline resolves or re-accommodates you under our Verdant Haven Quality Guarantee. Call +44 1451 820 441.
