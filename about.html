Our Heritage & Purpose

Reconnecting Modern Souls with the Peace of the Land

Verdant Haven was born from a simple belief: the most profound luxury in our hurried modern world is clean air, ancient trees, unpolluted starry skies, and warm hospitality shared around a wooden farm table.

The Founding Story

From an Old Cotswold Mill to a Global Sanctuary

In 2018, founder Eleanor Vance stepped away from high-pressure corporate design in London to restore her grandfather's stone barn in Gloucestershire. What began as a single weekend cabin hosting friends soon illuminated a universal hunger: city dwellers crave restorative silence and genuine connection with working farmers.

Today, Verdant Haven unites independent organic farmers, heritage bastide caretakers, vineyard owners, and eco-architects worldwide under a single standard of rustic luxury and ecological regeneration.

Our Milestones

The Verdant Haven Journey

2018

The First Stone Barn in Gloucestershire

Restoration of Old Mill Cottage using reclaimed Cotswold limestone, providing weary travelers their first taste of woodstove hospitality.

2020

Expanding to Tuscany & Black Forest

Partnering with family-run Italian vineyard estates and German solar-powered timber cabins to build our regenerative network.

2023

Launch of Hands-On Rural Activities

Inviting guests to participate in morning sourdough baking, goat milking, organic cider pressing, and telescope stargazing.

2026

Global Countryside SaaS & Guest Platform

Empowering independent rural hosts worldwide with modern reservation management while preserving timeless agrarian authenticity.
